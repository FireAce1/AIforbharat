# Village Coordinator Training Curriculum

## Overview

This 2-day training program prepares village coordinators to support KrishiAI adoption in their communities. Coordinators will learn to onboard farmers, troubleshoot common issues, and serve as the bridge between farmers and the KrishiAI team.

## Training Objectives

By the end of this training, coordinators will be able to:
1. Explain KrishiAI features and benefits to farmers
2. Help farmers download and set up the app
3. Demonstrate all key features
4. Troubleshoot common technical issues
5. Collect and report farmer feedback
6. Organize community training sessions

## Prerequisites

- Android smartphone (personal or provided)
- Basic smartphone usage skills
- Fluency in local language (Hindi/Marathi)
- Commitment to support farmers in their village

## Trainer Guide

### Trainer Qualifications
- Deep knowledge of KrishiAI platform
- Experience in adult education
- Fluency in Hindi/Marathi
- Agricultural background (preferred)
- Patience and empathy

### Training Delivery Tips

**Do's** ✅:
- Use simple, local language
- Provide hands-on practice
- Encourage questions
- Share real success stories
- Be patient with technology learning
- Adapt pace to group needs
- Use visual aids and demos
- Celebrate small wins

**Don'ts** ❌:
- Don't use technical jargon
- Don't rush through topics
- Don't skip hands-on practice
- Don't ignore struggling participants
- Don't make assumptions about tech literacy
- Don't criticize questions

### Handling Common Challenges

**Challenge**: Participants with low tech literacy
**Solution**: Pair with tech-savvy participants, provide extra one-on-one time

**Challenge**: Language barriers
**Solution**: Use local dialect, visual demonstrations, peer translation

**Challenge**: Skepticism about AI/technology
**Solution**: Show real results, farmer testimonials, emphasize offline functionality

**Challenge**: Concerns about data privacy
**Solution**: Explain security measures, show privacy policy, address specific concerns
