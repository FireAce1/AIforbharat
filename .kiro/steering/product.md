# Product Overview

## KrishiAI Platform

KrishiAI is an AI-powered rural development platform designed to democratize agricultural intelligence for 150M+ Indian farmers. The platform aims to increase farmer income by 40%, reduce waste by 30%, and build climate resilience through data-driven decision support.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Offline-First**: Works without internet connectivity
- **Voice-First**: Supports 12+ Indian languages with voice input/output
- **Mobile-First**: Optimized for low-end Android devices (2GB RAM)
- **Data-Light**: Less than 5MB monthly data usage
- **Freemium Model**: Core features free for small farmers (< 2 hectares)

## Key Modules

1. **AI Crop Intelligence**: Crop recommendations, disease detection, yield prediction
2. **Market Intelligence**: Real-time price forecasting, direct farmer-to-buyer marketplace
3. **Climate Intelligence**: Weather prediction, water optimization, sustainability recommendations
4. **Government Services**: Multilingual chatbot, scheme discovery, application assistance
5. **Community Support**: Village dashboards, FPO tools, peer learning

## Target Users

- Primary: Small/marginal farmers (< 2 hectares) - 86% of Indian farmers
- Secondary: Village leaders, FPO coordinators, agricultural extension officers
- Geographic Focus: Rural India, starting with Maharashtra, MP, UP, Punjab, Karnataka

## Success Metrics

- User adoption: 50M farmers by Year 5
- Income increase: 40% average
- Water savings: 35% average
- Yield improvement: 18%
- Post-harvest loss reduction: 50%
